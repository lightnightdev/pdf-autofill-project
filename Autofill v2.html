Tips / Shortcuts

Placing a marker:
- Choose a column on the right
- Click to place a marker
- Click the column again to duplicate it

Editing a marker:
- Click an existing marker or its column card
- Use (+/=) and (-) keys to adjust font size
- Use arrow keys to move
- Press (Delete) to delete marker

Unzipping on Windows:
- Right click file > Properties > Unblock
